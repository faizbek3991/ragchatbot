# IT Security Guide for Staff

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of letters, numbers and symbols. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Never reuse your last five passwords.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud applications. Use the approved authenticator app on your company phone.

## Reporting incidents
Report any suspected phishing email or lost laptop to the IT helpdesk within one hour at helpdesk@example.com or extension 2020. Do not forward the suspicious email to colleagues.

## Data handling
Confidential files must be stored only in the approved company drive. Personal USB drives are not allowed on company laptops.
